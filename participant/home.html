Abstract & poster submission guidelines

Abstract format

  • Submit as a Word document (.doc/.docx) only — maximum 300 words, single paragraph.
  • Include title, author(s), affiliation, and up to 5 keywords.
  • Select the most relevant theme from the 15 listed on the submission page.
  • Deadline: 20 November 2026 — no edits are possible after submission.

Oral presentation

  • 7 minutes for presentation + 3 minutes for discussion (10 minutes total).
  • Upload your final .pptx after abstract acceptance — no changes permitted post-submission.
  • Recommended: 8–10 slides, 16:9 widescreen format.

Poster presentation

  • Size: A1 portrait (594 × 841 mm), printed and brought in person.
  • Include title, authors, affiliation, and a VRMIC ID/QR tag at the top.
  • No file upload needed here — only your abstract is submitted online.

General notes

  • All accepted abstracts are published in the conference proceedings (ISBN).
  • Selected papers may be invited for Scopus/WoS-indexed journal publication.
  • Contact the organising committee for any correction requests after submission.
