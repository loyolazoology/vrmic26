About the conference

Bringing together entomologists, public health scientists, and policy makers to address vector-borne disease risk and environmental sustainability.

Who should attend

Faculty, researchers, students, government vector-control officials, and industry professionals working in life sciences and environmental health.

Formats

Keynote sessions, oral paper presentations, and poster presentations — submission details on the Call for Papers page.
