  Indian Academy of Environmental Science

Medals, Certificates & Honorary Fellowships

The IAES recognises active researchers, academicians and environmental activists at every career stage — encouraging young scientists on one hand, and honouring senior researchers in eco-biological and environmental disciplines on the other.

  Eligibility

Medals and Awards of the Academy

The IAES has introduced a number of medals, certificates and honorary fellowships for active researchers, academicians and environmental activists, specially related to myriad aspects of eco-biological science. These recognitions have carved a niche for emerging, active and established scientists at different age groups — encouraging young researchers on one hand, and giving due recognition to senior researchers in the related disciplines on the other.

  Categories

The awards

  1. Fellow of Indian Academy of Environmental Science (FIAES) Certificate of Fellowship.
  2. IAES Gold Medal — Senior Category More than twenty years of teaching / research experience.
  3. Academy's Gold Medal — Emerging / Young Scientist Category For early-career researchers making a notable mark in the field.
  4. Silver Jubilee Gold Medal More than ten years of teaching / research experience.
  5. Swami Maheshanand Girijimaharaj Gold Medal Above ten years of experience.
  6. Dr. K.K. Tyagi Memorial Gold Medal Above ten years of experience.
  7. Dr. S. A. Salgare Gold Medal Above ten years of experience.
  8. Prof. Shasikala Belsare Gold Medal Above ten years of experience.
  9. Prof. B. N. Pandey Gold Medal Above ten years of experience.
  10. Prof. P.C. Joshi Gold Medal For research on myriad aspects of Forest Ecology and Entomology.
